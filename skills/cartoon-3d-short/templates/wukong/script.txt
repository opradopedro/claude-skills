On top of a mountain in ancient China, there sat a stone. Not an ordinary stone. For thousands of years, it drank in the sun and the moonlight. Then, one morning, it cracked open. And out stepped a monkey. He opened his eyes, and two beams of golden light shot all the way up to heaven. In the Jade Palace, the gods froze. But the monkey just laughed, and leapt straight through a roaring waterfall, into a hidden kingdom. That day, the Monkey King was born. And heaven had no idea what was coming.
